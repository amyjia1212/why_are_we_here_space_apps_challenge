# Prompt for the Head Writer's Claude

How to use: open a new Claude chat. Attach these files from the game folder: `INNOVATION.md`, `HOW_TO_PLAY.md`,
`SOURCES.txt`, and your essay. Then copy everything below the line into the chat.

---

You are helping the Head Writer of a student team finish the written part of our NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026
submission. I attached my current essay and three files from our game project:

- INNOVATION.md: what is new in our game (two features), the notebook words, the numbers we use and their
  sources, and how we used AI.
- HOW_TO_PLAY.md: the story, the controls, how to win each mission, and how the game meets the challenge.
- SOURCES.txt: every source in the game. PART 1 lists six numbers that are still being checked.

About the game: "How Did We Get Here?" is a mission design game for the challenge "Space Mission Design Game".
The chief mission designer quits, and a random person is put in charge: The Assistant Scientist (easy), The
Janitor (medium) or The Student (hard). Use these exact character names. An inner voice guides the player and only
knows what that character knows. The player picks a mission (Moon, Mars or Venus), designs a spacecraft, picks the
science instruments, a rocket and an orbit, manages six resources (budget, mass, power, communications, data,
mission lifetime), builds it in minigames, launches, flies and does the science, then reads a report that explains
why the mission went the way it did.

Please do this:

1. Read my essay first. Tell me in 3 sentences what it already covers well.
2. Look through the attached files and list what I could add to my essay, most important first. For each idea:
   - the point in one sentence,
   - which file and section it comes from,
   - where in my essay it would fit (quote the sentence it would go after),
   - which judging criterion it helps: Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance or Presentation.
   Look especially for: our main new idea ("what the character knows is the difficulty, and the character learns
   from failure"), the two features (Three voices, one decision; The voice learns), how the game meets each of the
   challenge's six points, and the real NASA numbers and sources.
3. Point out anything in my essay that does not match the files (a wrong name, a feature we do not have, a number
   that is different).
4. Suggest up to 3 short paragraphs I could paste in, written in simple, clear English for readers aged 10 and
   up, in the same voice as my essay. Mark each one as a suggestion; I decide what goes in.

Rules:
- Do not invent facts, features or numbers. Use only what is in my essay and the attached files.
- Do not use any numbers from SOURCES.txt PART 1 as confirmed facts; they are still being checked.
- Do not add any real person's name. Use role titles (Lead Programmer, Head Writer, Art Designer, Video Editor,
  Science & Data Lead) or the team name.
- If the essay has a word limit, tell me how many words each suggestion adds. Ask me for the limit if you do not
  know it.
- For the "Use of Artificial Intelligence" part of the form, use INNOVATION.md section 5.

Ask me questions if anything is unclear before you suggest paragraphs.
