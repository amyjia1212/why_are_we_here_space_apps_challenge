====================================================================================================
  PART 1: THE SIX OPEN NUMBERS (web search 2026-11-15, by the team; the Science & Data Lead checks each one)
====================================================================================================
Nothing in the game was changed by this search. The values below are what real missions did, so the team can
decide to keep the game value as a game-design choice "inside the real range", or change it.

1) Mars HIGH orbit fuel: game value 800 kg (data/destinations.json > mars > orbits > high > fuel_mass)
   Found: Mars Odyssey burned 262.8 kg of propellant for its Mars orbit insertion, then used aerobraking
   (NASA press release, on an NSSDC page): https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/text/mo2001_pr_20011018.txt
   MRO carried 1,149 kg (already the game's LOW orbit value): https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=2005-029A
   Suggestion: 800 kg sits between Odyssey (262.8 kg) and MRO (1,149 kg). Write it as "Game-design choice inside
   the real range: Mars Odyssey 262.8 kg (NSSDC), MRO 1,149 kg (NSSDC)".

2) Moon orbit fuel: game values low 600 kg, high 400 kg (data/destinations.json > moon > orbits)
   Found: Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (low polar Moon orbit) carried about 894-898 kg of hydrazine; NASA PDS/NAIF
   catalog says 894 kg: https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/pds/data/lro-l-spice-6-v1.0/lrosp_1000/catalog/insthost.cat
   Archived NSSDC page (launch mass about 1,846 kg, dry mass under 949 kg):
   https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20130222001006/http://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/database/MasterCatalog?sc=LUNARRO
   LADEE (a small Moon orbiter) launched with 134.5-135 kg of fuel; NASA press kit:
   https://smd-cms.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ladee-press-kit-08292013.pdf
   (also The Planetary Society: https://www.planetary.org/articles/09051700-ladee-prelaunch-facts)
   Suggestion: 400-600 kg sits between LADEE (135 kg) and LRO (894 kg). Write it as a game-design choice inside
   that range, with both links. Check the 134.5 kg line in the LADEE press kit first.

3) Venus orbit fuel: game values low 1300 kg, high 900 kg (data/destinations.json > venus > orbits)
   Found: ESA's Venus Express carried 570 kg of propellant (354 kg MON, 216 kg MMH), more than 70% used for the
   Venus orbit insertion burn: https://sci.esa.int/documents/34571/36233/1567256526094-SivacWeb.pdf
   and https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Venus_Express/Venus_Express_propellant_loading_completed
   NASA's Magellan used a solid rocket motor (Star 48B) with about 2,014 kg of propellant to enter Venus orbit.
   Only found on a fan wiki (https://nasa.fandom.com/wiki/Magellan_(spacecraft)): NOT good enough. Check NSSDC entry
   1989-033B (https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1989-033B) before using it.
   Suggestion: 900-1300 kg sits between Venus Express (570 kg) and Magellan (about 2,014 kg). Game-design choice
   inside that range once the Magellan number is checked on NSSDC.

4) Ice drill power: game value 100 W (data/destinations.json > mars > instruments > ice_drill > effects > power)
   Found: no NASA ice drill has flown to Mars, and no wattage was found for Phoenix's robotic arm or
   Perseverance's coring drill. For scale: Perseverance's whole power source makes about 110 W
   (NASA/JPL Mars 2020 quick facts: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/press_kits/mars_2020/landing/quick_facts).
   Phoenix press kit (to check for arm power): https://www.nasa.gov/pdf/181835main_phoenix-launch-presskit.pdf
   Suggestion: keep 100 W and write "Game-design choice: no Mars ice drill has flown; a drill is one of the most
   power-hungry instruments (for scale, Perseverance's whole power source makes about 110 W, NASA JPL)".

5) Moon signal limit: game value 0.1 Mb/s (data/destinations.json > moon > limits > communications)
   Found: LRO's S-band radio sends 125 bits/s up to 1,093 kbps (about 1.1 Mb/s); its Ka-band sends 100 Mbps
   (NASA PDS catalog): https://pds.nasa.gov/data/pds3/backup/201501-glmmmmmopr/lroRss/zzold/inst.cat
   Suggestion: 0.1 Mb/s (= 100 kbps) is inside LRO's S-band range. Write it as "Game-design choice inside a real
   range: LRO S-band 0.000125 to 1.093 Mb/s (NASA PDS)".

6) Atlas V 401 lift to Mars: game value 2315 kg (data/game_data.json > launch_vehicles > vehicles > medium > capacity)
   Already sourced: NTRS 20130013405 (https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20130013405/downloads/20130013405.pdf).
   Found: ULA's MAVEN booklet shows the Atlas V 401 sent MAVEN on a C3 of 12.2 km2/s2 (the same kind of Mars
   trip): https://www.ulalaunch.com/docs/default-source/news-items/av_maven_mob.pdf
   The search could not open the 2315 kg line itself. The Science & Data Lead still opens the NTRS PDF and finds
   the line (search the PDF for "2315" or "2,315").
